The bot's characters

One character writes each reply, picked by what the message is (a greeting, a question, a joke...). Someone who is chatting with the bot keeps the same character for two minutes, and your own talk always goes to ChatBuddy. They are never all active at once.

Teach the bot

Give a thumbs up to replies you like and a thumbs down to ones you don't: the bot copies the style of the first and avoids the second. It also keeps notes on the stream and on regular viewers, and learns which of its characters get chat talking. All of it stays on this PC; you can wipe it below.
